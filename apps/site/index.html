What you get

Built to stop the wrong payment.

Live on Sepolia, powered by real Ethereum mainnet data.

  • Every common payment, checked twice. ETH payments and token transfer / transferFrom calls are checked before you sign and again at execution.
  • Hidden calls stopped. The Guard refuses delegatecall, so a payment can’t be tucked inside a batch.
  • Lookalikes refused on-chain. Same first 4 and last 4 characters as an approved payee, different address: the Guard reverts the payment.
  • Real data at real scale. The detector found 47,694 suspected poisoning records in 7,200 Ethereum mainnet blocks, about a day of USDT and USDC transfers.
  • Private by design. Recipients are compared in your browser, never looked up on a server, and Safe reads go through the Nox mixnet by default.

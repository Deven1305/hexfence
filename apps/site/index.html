How it works

A warning can be clicked through. The Guard can’t. Open evidence powers the warning; an on-chain rule makes the final call.

  1. 01 Observe

    Detector

    Reads Ethereum mainnet USDT and USDC transfer logs for address-poisoning patterns.

  2. 02 Publish

    Arkiv evidence

    Publishes open records anyone can re-check against Ethereum, each labelled suspected.

  3. 03 Explore

    Evidence explorer

    Browse suspected records and inspect their linked Ethereum receipts.

  4. 04 Compare

    Address X-Ray

    Read the full recipient in groups of four, with matching and different groups highlighted.

  5. 05 Check

    HexFence Pay verdict

    Checks every recipient in your browser against your approved payees and the public evidence, before you sign.

  6. 06 Enforce

    HexFenceGuard

    Refuses a payment to any different address that shares an approved payee’s fingerprint, directly on your Safe.

Nothing to install: no wallet, no account, no backend. Your browser loads the published records. Check any address privately: the check runs on your own machine.

Built to stop the wrong payment.

  • Every common payment, checked twice. ETH payments and token transfer / transferFrom calls are checked before you sign and again at execution.
  • Hidden calls stopped. The Guard refuses delegatecall, so a payment can’t be tucked inside a batch.
  • Lookalikes refused on-chain. Same first 4 and last 4 characters as an approved payee, different address: the Guard reverts the payment.
  • Other calls are not checked. HexFence checks only ETH and ERC-20 transfers; anything else shows “cannot verify”.
  • Private by design. Recipients are compared in your browser, never looked up on a server, and Safe reads go through the Nox mixnet by default.
